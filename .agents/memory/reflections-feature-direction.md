name: Reflections feature + monetization stance
description: Founder's stance on survivors monetizing their own recovery story
---

The founder (Nick, stroke survivor) **rejects "don't monetize trauma."** It's his trauma — monetizing his hard-won lived experience to help others is wanted and right, not exploitation.

**Rule:** In any Reflections / story-to-product / monetization UX, keep monetization **proud and first-class** — "your story, your royalties." Survivors turning entries into poems / chapters / books (Lulu) / anthems can share free OR sell, and keep their earnings. Foreground earnings with dignity; do NOT hide them in a footer.

The ONLY place to stay gentle: how the journaling tool greets a **brand-new survivor** (no pressure to "perform" recovery to sell before they've processed). That nuance is about *them*, not the founder.

**Dual structure:** Stroke Lyfe Inc (nonprofit, reach + give-back) runs alongside the for-profit (livelihood). Weave in an **optional** give-back thread (route a portion of royalties to Stroke Lyfe), framed as a virtuous loop — never guilt.

**Why:** corrected an earlier over-cautious "quiet monetization" framing that landed wrong.
**How to apply:** when building Reflections or any survivor-publishing/earnings surface, lead with dignity + pride in earning, not apology.
