## Frequently asked questions

### What is Artlist?

Artlist is the ultimate platform for video creators, providing the best AI tools and highest-quality creative assets made by leading artists worldwide. We offer AI image and video generators, AI voiceover, royalty-free music for videos, [sound effects](https://artlist.io/sfx), [stock footage](https://artlist.io/stock-footage), [video templates](https://artlist.io/video-templates), [LUTs](https://artlist.io/luts), and video-editing plugins and extensions.

### Are Artlist assets royalty-free?

Artlist’s digital assets are all royalty-free, meaning that you can use them in your videos without having to worry about [music licensing](https://artlist.io/royalty-free-music) or paying royalty fees.

### Can I try Artlist for free?

Yes. With a free account, you can generate images, videos, and voiceovers for free and access watermarked songs and footage, and save your favorite assets in Arboards. To download unlimited unwatermarked assets and use them in your projects, you’ll need a paid subscription.

### What plans does Artlist offer, and what do they cover?

Artlist offers several plan options: [AI Suite](https://artlist.io/page/pricing/max?ai_suite_modal=1), [AI Voiceover](https://artlist.io/voice-over?ai_suite_modal=1), [Music & SFX](https://artlist.io/page/pricing/max?music_sfx_modal=1), [Footage & Templates](https://artlist.io/page/pricing/max?footage_templates_modal=1), and [Artlist Max](https://artlist.io/page/pricing/max) — an all-in-one subscription with unlimited access to every AI tool and asset in the catalog. You can choose a Pro license, which covers all types of video worldwide (including commercial use), or a personal Social license for social media channels only.

### What is the business plan, and what does it cover?

Artlist’s Business plan is built for teams creating at scale, with the robust security and freedom you need. It includes a Business license that covers projects on every platform, anywhere. You’ll get a multi-user account with seats you can add any time. The plan includes cutting-edge [AI image generators](https://artlist.io/text-to-image-ai) and [video generation](https://artlist.io/image-to-video-ai), [AI voiceovers](https://artlist.io/voice-over), video-editing plugins, and unlimited access to music, sound effects, footage, templates, and LUTs. Learn more about the [Business plan](https://artlist.io/enterprise).

### How many digital assets are available on Artlist?

Artlist offers over 900K+ digital assets for video creators, including royalty-free music, sound effects, stock footage, video templates, and LUTs. Our catalog includes original content that is exclusive to Artlist, and it is updated with fresh assets daily. On Artlist, you can find the perfect asset for every project need, whether you find it or generate it with AI.

### What types of AI generation tools does Artlist offer?

Artlist offers AI video generation, including [text-to-video](https://artlist.io/text-to-video-ai) and image-to-video; AI image generation and editing, including text-to-image and [image-to-image](https://artlist.io/image-to-image-ai); and AI voiceover generation, including text-to-voice, [voice-to-voice](https://artlist.io/voice-over/voice-to-voice), and [voice cloning](https://artlist.io/voice-over/voice-cloning). Every AI tool is powered by the most advanced models available today.

### Do I have the rights to AI-generated content? Can I use it for commercial purposes?

Yes. Any content you generate with Artlist comes with full rights — you can use, adapt, distribute and monetize it without restriction, limited only by our License and [Terms of Use](https://artlist.io/help-center/privacy-terms/terms-of-use). Artlist has no claim over your AI outputs. Just be sure your content respects third-party rights such as trademarks, intellectual property, and personal likeness.

### Is my generated content used to train AI models?

No. Artlist and its partners never use your prompts, uploads or outputs to train AI models. Everything you create is yours alone, protected by strict security standards and accessible only to you.
