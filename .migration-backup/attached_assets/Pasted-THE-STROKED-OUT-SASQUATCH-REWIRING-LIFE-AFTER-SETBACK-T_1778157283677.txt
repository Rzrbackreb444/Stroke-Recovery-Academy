INTRODUCTION: FROM “NEVER” TO “NOW” (3,500 words)
“You’ll probably never walk normally again.” That prognosis hit me like a freight train on December 3, 2018, after a hemorrhagic stroke left me paralyzed, skull stapled with 50, at age 36. The U.S. sees 795,000 strokes yearly, claiming 165,393 lives (AHA 2025) [9], but I rewired to 90% function, defying odds. This book merges my 20+ manuscripts into the ultimate recovery guide, outstripping Stronger After Stroke with lived truth, 2025 science, and universal power.
Strokes vary: ischemic (87%, clots, CDC 2025) [0], hemorrhagic (10–15%, bleeds like mine), TIAs. Drop foot plagues 20–30% (AHA 2025) [19]; AFOs aid 70% (Journal of Rehabilitation 2025) [59]. Caregivers face 40% burnout (AHA 2025) [19]. Beyond stroke, this applies to divorce, addiction, bankruptcy. Neuroplasticity thrives with repetition (Frontiers 2025) [39], 66 days forge habits (Lally, 2009; 18–254 range, Healthline 2025) [32], and my EMT activates 80% motor circuits (Decety & Jeannerod, 1995) [39].
