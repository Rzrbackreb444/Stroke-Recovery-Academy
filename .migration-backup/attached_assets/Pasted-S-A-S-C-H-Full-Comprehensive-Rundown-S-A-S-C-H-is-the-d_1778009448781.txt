S.A.S.C.H. – Full Comprehensive Rundown
S.A.S.C.H. is the daily operating system for stroke recovery and life reconstruction created by Nick Kremers, The Stroked-Out Sasquatch.
It stands for:
•  S = Seize the Day (Ownership & Intent)
•  A = Absorb the Lesson (Reflection & Learning)
•  S = Swing the Work (Action & Repetition)
•  C = Combine Elements (Experimentation & Adaptation)
•  H = Hammer It Home (Reflection, Stacking & Reinforcement)
What S.A.S.C.H. Really Means
S.A.S.C.H. turns recovery from something passive and medical into active daily construction work. You are not waiting to be healed. You are the architect and the hammer building a new version of yourself with whatever is left after the stroke.
•  Seize the Day Take full ownership every morning. No one is coming to save you. Start with clear intent and gratitude. Remind yourself this is your second chance. Ask: “What do I still have? What am I building today?”
•  Absorb the Lesson Every flicker, every small win, every failure is data. Reflect honestly at the end of the day. What worked? What didn’t? Turn everything into learning instead of self-pity or defeat.
•  Swing the Work Do the reps even when it hurts. Physical movement, stretching, exercise, self-care (teeth brushing, grooming, showering with adaptations) — swing the hammer daily. “Chop wood” no matter how boring or painful the day feels. Use the Sasquatch persona to push through: “The Sasquatch doesn’t skip the rep.”
•  Combine Elements Experiment and adapt. Mix techniques. Try mirror therapy with the baseball bat, mental rehearsal with physical reps, different angles or tools. Don’t follow rigid protocols. Combine what actually works for your body and deficits.
•  Hammer It Home Reinforce what you learned. Stack wins. Reframe failures as data. End each day by logging progress and asking: “What did I build today? What will I fix tomorrow?” Make the day’s work permanent through reflection and gratitude.

Practical Daily Use (66-Day Foundation)
Every day follows the S.A.S.C.H. rhythm:
•  Morning: Seize + Absorb — self-care (teeth brushing, grooming, showering with adaptations) + gratitude (“This is my second chance”).
•  Midday: Swing + Combine — stretching, exercise, movement using the Sasquatch persona to push through.
•  Evening: Hammer — reflection, log wins, reframe failures as data, stack progress.
