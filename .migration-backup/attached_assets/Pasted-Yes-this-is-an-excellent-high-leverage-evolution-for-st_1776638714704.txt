Recommendations for Implementation
1.  Structure the 66-Day Program
	•  Core Framework: Tie every day to S.A.S.C.H. (Seize the day, Absorb knowledge/lessons, Swing/do the work, Combine elements, Hammer it home with reflection).
	•  Daily Routine Template (simple, repeatable, adaptable for different deficits/levels):
		•  Morning Anchor (wake-up ritual): Gratitude moment (“What do I still have? This is my second chance.”) + basic self-care (teeth brushing, grooming, showering with adaptive tips).
		•  Movement Block: Stretching + exercise (seated/standing options, weight-bearing, using persona: “Sasquatch doesn’t skip the rep”).
		•  Persona Push: Use the alter-ego to tackle one hard thing (e.g., “Phoenix rises and does the shower even when tired”).
		•  Stacking Wins/Failures: End-of-day reflection — log 1-3 successes (even tiny ones like “brushed teeth with weak hand”) and reframe 1 failure (“Missed stretch → data for tomorrow’s adjustment”).
		•  Evening Wind-Down: Light review + blessing mindset (“Today I built a little more of my new life”).
	•  Progression: Week 1-2 focus on basics (hygiene, simple movement). Week 3-6 layer in consistency and persona strength. Week 7-9+ emphasize stacking and seeing the “new life” as opportunity.
	•  Adaptability: Provide levels (seated vs standing, minimal vs full energy) and disclaimers (“All strokes differ — this is a blueprint; consult your team”).
2.  Mindset Layer (The Real Differentiator)
	•  Teach “stacking successes” daily (celebrate flickers) and “stack failures into successes” (every miss = data + adjustment, not defeat).
	•  Frame the “new life” as a blessing/second chance: Use your story (wheelchair to holding son with both hands) as proof. Tools like daily gratitude prompts or “What am I building today?” journal.
	•  Integrate the persona heavily here — it’s the tool for pushing through when depression or “what I lost” hits.
3.  How to Package & Monetize on the Site

4.  Content & Features to Add

	•  Community: Dedicated “66-Day Crew” forum for sharing routines and wins.
