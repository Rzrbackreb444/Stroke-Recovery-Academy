🧮 Core Modules

1. CLEANBI Valuation Engine

• Inputs: ZIP, rent, lease terms, equipment mix, store size, utility bills
• Outputs: CLEANBI score, A–C grade, valuation range, Gemini summary
• PDF export: Mapbox visuals + Gemini narrative + FSBO readiness


2. Drag-and-Drop Equipment Calculator

• Canvas: Interactive layout builder
• Modules: Washer/dryer by brand, size, spec
• Logic: Revenue delta, space usage, utility load, CLEANBI impact
• Gemini: Suggests optimal mix based on demographics + store profile


3. Optimal Dashboard

• Multi-store metrics: TPD, utility %, labor %, net margin
• SOP builder: Gemini-generated checklists, training guides
• Lease analyzer: Flags risk, scores terms, suggests negotiation levers


4. Blog Suite (SEO-Optimized)

• MDX-powered posts
• JSON-LD schema for articles, authors, FAQs
• Dynamic sitemap
• Gemini-assisted summaries, intros, meta descriptions
• Embedded lead capture: valuation teaser + email opt-in

🧭 CLEANBI Grading System

Grade	Score Range	Meaning	
A	90–100	Top 5% of locations. Premium valuation, ideal for scaling or sale.	
B	75–89	Solid investment. Minor improvements unlock major upside.	
C	60–74	Workable but risky. Needs lease, equipment, or location upgrades.	
D	<60	High-risk. Only viable with deep discount or repositioning.	


Gemini generates a full SWOT analysis, lease health breakdown, and optimization roadmap per grade.
