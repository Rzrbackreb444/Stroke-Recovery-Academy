2. The S.A.S.C.H. Formula (Expanded Cheat Sheet – Pure Text)
The S.A.S.C.H. Formula
Sasquatch Action System – Construction Hammer
Your daily recovery operating system. Five powerful steps. No fluff. No zero days.
1. S – Seize
Accept exactly where you are right now. No denial, no wishing it was different. This is your raw material. Be brutally honest about your current reality — your flickers, your limitations, your starting point. You can’t build on fantasy.
2. A – Absorb
Take in every useful piece of knowledge you can find. Learn from therapists, science, other survivors, videos, books, and especially your own body’s feedback. Be a sponge. Absorb without judgment.
3. S – Swing
Experiment. Test. Take action. Try new angles, tools, or reps even if they feel awkward or useless at first. Swing the bat. Do the mirror work. Shift your weight. Send the signal. Action beats perfection.
4. C – Combine
Mix the best pieces together into your own custom system. Don’t follow one method blindly. Take what worked from today’s swing and blend it with other tools. Build your personal recovery recipe.
5. H – Hammer
Apply it relentlessly every single day. No zero days. Hammer when you’re tired. Hammer when progress feels invisible. Hammer when motivation is gone. Consistency is the hammer that builds empires.
Daily Quick Checklist
•  Seize: I accepted where I am today
•  Absorb: I took in new knowledge or feedback
•  Swing: I experimented or took action
•  Combine: I mixed what worked into my system
•  Hammer: I did at least one rep — no zero day
