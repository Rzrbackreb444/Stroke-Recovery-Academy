# Research Notes: Stroke Recovery Platform Demand and Revenue

**Status:** researching
**Depth:** Deep

## Plan

- **Question:** What do stroke survivors, caregivers, therapists, and clinicians need from digital recovery products, and can this platform become a meaningful business without overpromising medical outcomes?
- **Scope:** Search demand, recovery needs, digital-health evidence, stakeholder workflows, competitive positioning, monetization constraints, and fit with the current Academy.
- **Audience:** Product owner and founder deciding the next platform investment.
- **Deliverable:** Evidence-backed market and product-direction report with source links, prioritized recommendations, and realistic revenue scenarios.
