Our individual apps cost between $10 and $30 USD + tax. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription or recurring cost. You own the app and will receive free updates as they are released.

You can **see the cost** of each app in your country by **going directly** to the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and checking the price there. You can also find USD, CAD, GBP, AUS, and EUR prices on our free downloadable [brochures](https://tactustherapy.com/brochure/).

## For customers outside the USA

Apple and Google convert the US$ prices into local currency prices according to their own policies, not daily conversion rates. We have no control over prices in other countries. If the country you are in requires sales tax to be added at checkout, Apple and Google will automatically do this. Some prices already include VAT or other taxes. You should always check the final price before buying.

## Discounts

While Tactus Therapy apps are already value-priced, there are even more ways to save when you buy in bulk:

- Some apps are already bundled together (4-in-1) to offer savings of 25% over buying each app individually.
- We also offer [App Bundles for iOS users](https://tactustherapy.com/tactus-bundles/), combining multiple apps together for even greater savings.
- Educational institutions can qualify for 50% off on purchases of 20 or more licenses. [https://volume.itunes.apple.com/us/store](https://volume.itunes.apple.com/us/store)
- Considering a large purchase (10+ copies of all our apps) for your hospital or healthcare company? [Contact us](https://help.tactustherapy.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) to discuss options.
- Universities, researchers, non-profit aphasia centers, and speakers may qualify for free apps.
