# Cerebrovascular Disease or Stroke

Cerebrovascular Disease or Stroke

Related Pages

Data are for the U.S.

Morbidity

- Number of adults who ever had a stroke: 7.8 million
- Percent of adults who ever had a stroke: 3.1%

Source: [Summary Health Statistics Tables for U.S. Adults: National Health Interview Survey, 2018, Tables A-1b, A-1c \[PDF – 137 KB\]](https://ftp.cdc.gov/pub/Health_Statistics/NCHS/NHIS/SHS/2018_SHS_Table_A-1.pdf)

Physician office visits

- Number of visits to physician offices with stroke as the primary diagnosis: 2.2 million

Source: [National Ambulatory Medical Care Survey: 2016 National Summary Tables, table 15](https://archive.cdc.gov/#/details?url=https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/ahcd/namcs_summary/2016_namcs_web_tables.pdf)

Emergency department visits

- Number of visits to emergency departments with stroke as the primary diagnosis: 686,000

Source: [National Hospital Ambulatory Medical Care Survey: 2022 National Summary Tables, table 11 \[PDF – 1 MB\]](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhamcs/web_tables/2022-nhamcs-ed-web-tables.pdf)

Mortality

- Number of deaths: 166,852
- Deaths per 100,000 population: 49.1
- Cause of death rank: 4

Source: [National Vital Statistics System – Mortality Data (2024) via CDC WONDER](http://wonder.cdc.gov/controller/saved/D158/D470F661)
