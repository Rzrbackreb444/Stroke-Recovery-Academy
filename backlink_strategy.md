# Backlink Strategy — Stroke Recovery Academy

## Objective
Move from 0 backlinks to 50+ quality backlinks in 90 days, targeting stroke recovery niche authority, health/wellness, and disability advocacy sites.

## Current State
- Domain: strokerecoveryacademy.com (launched 2024)
- Backlinks: 0 tracked
- Domain Authority: 0
- Indexed pages: 48 (SSR blog posts + DB posts)
- 15,000 daily TikTok views — social proof exists, not yet converted to links

## Phase 1: Linkable Assets (Weeks 1-4)

### Asset 1: Original Research — "The 7-Year Stroke Recovery Timeline"
- Survey of 500+ survivors on the Academy platform
- Data: what equipment worked, what did not, cost vs. outcome, time to milestones
- Format: long-form blog post + downloadable infographic
- Target: health journalists, stroke foundations, rehab clinics
- Pitch: "The largest survivor-led equipment study ever published"

### Asset 2: Infographic — "The Stroke Recovery Equipment Map"
- Visual flowchart: acute → subacute → chronic → long-term
- Equipment at each stage with cost, evidence level, and survivor rating
- Format: PNG + embeddable HTML snippet
- Target: Pinterest, stroke survivor blogs, rehab clinic resource pages

### Asset 3: Statistics Compilation — "101 Stroke Recovery Statistics"
- Curated from peer-reviewed journals + Academy survey data
- Cite every source — builds trust, earns links from researchers
- Format: blog post with anchor links to each stat
- Target: health writers, medical students, content marketers

## Phase 3: Reddit + Forum Participation (Weeks 1-12)

### Communities
- r/stroke (200k+ members) — answer equipment questions, link to relevant blog posts
- r/strokerecovery (50k+ members) — daily engagement, share progress
- r/disability (500k+ members) — cross-post equipment guides
- r/AskDocs (200k+ members) — answer stroke-related questions, build authority
- Stroke Network forums (strokenetwork.org) — equipment reviews
- Recovery.com forums — long-form answers with links

### Rules
- Never spam — answer the question first, link second
- Only link when the blog post adds genuine value beyond the comment
- Disclose affiliation — "I run Stroke Recovery Academy, and we reviewed this"
- Track which posts drive traffic and double down

## Phase 4: HARO + Expert Quotes (Weeks 1-12)

### HARO Pitches
- Sign up for HARO (Help A Reporter Out) — journalist queries 3x daily
- Filter: health, disability, fitness, aging
- Respond within 2 hours with a quote + link
- Track: which outlets publish the quote and link back

### Target Queries
- "Stroke recovery experts — what equipment works?"
- "Survivor stories — rebuilding after paralysis"
- "Home gym for disabled athletes — recommendations?"
- "Supplements for brain health — evidence-based?"

## Phase 5: Community Outreach (Weeks 4-12)

### Stroke Foundations
- Offer to write the "equipment guide" page for their resource section
- Offer to speak at their virtual events
- Offer to run a free webinar: "The 5 Things I Wish I Knew at Month One"

### Rehab Clinics
- Send the equipment guide to 50 outpatient rehab clinics
- Ask: "Would you share this with your discharge patients?"
- Track: which clinics add the link to their discharge packet

### Universities
- Contact OT/PT programs at 20 universities
- Offer: "I will speak to your class about survivor-led recovery"
- Result: .edu links from department pages, speaker bios
