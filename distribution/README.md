# Distribution Kit — Stroke Recovery Academy

This is how you get the first humans onto the blog without a list, without ad
spend, and without turning into a spammer. It's built for one person doing this
by hand, thirty minutes a day.

Heart's still thumpin'. Let's get to work.

## The five rules — break these and you get banned, not subscribers

1. **Be a member first.** Comment, answer, encourage for a week before you ever
   drop a link. Ratio: give ten times, link once.
2. **Lead with the help, not the URL.** Answer the question in the comment
   itself. The link is a "if you want the longer version" afterthought — never
   the whole reply.
3. **Disclose.** Say it plain: "I run a stroke-recovery site" and "some of the
   product links are affiliate — I earn a small cut, costs you nothing." People
   forgive honesty. They never forgive a hidden pitch.
4. **You are not a doctor.** Neither am I. Every time gear, meds, or symptoms
   come up: "run it by your doctor / PT / OT." No exceptions. This is a
   your-money-your-life topic and one bad take sinks the whole thing.
5. **Read the sub's rules before posting.** Most stroke groups restrict links and
   self-promo. Some allow it on certain days or in your profile only. Respect it.
   When links are banned, help anyway and put the site in your profile.

## Where to show up

- **Reddit:** r/stroke, r/stroke_survivors, r/braininjury, r/caregivers
- **Facebook:** search "stroke survivor," "stroke recovery," "stroke caregiver"
  groups. The big ones have tens of thousands of members and real daily traffic.
- **Quora:** answer "stroke recovery" questions — evergreen, ranks in Google.
- **YouTube/TikTok comments:** on stroke-recovery videos, be the helpful reply.

## Cadence — thirty minutes, most days

- 20 min: answer 3–5 questions with real help (no link, or link only if it
  genuinely fits and the sub allows it).
- 5 min: one value post per week per platform (templates in `posts.md`).
- 5 min: reply to anyone who responded to you. Relationships > reach.

## What NOT to do

- No copy-paste blasting the same post across ten groups in one hour.
- No DMing strangers your link.
- No "cure" language, no fake before/afters, no invented stats.
- No leading with the affiliate product. Lead with the person.
